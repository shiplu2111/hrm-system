## 1. Scope & Acceptance

By activating a trial or paid subscription workspace on CMSN-HR ("Service"), the subscribing organization ("Customer") agrees to the current Terms of Service version recorded during workspace registration.

## 2. Multi-Tenant Workspace & Accounts

- Each Customer receives an isolated tenant workspace (`subdomain` or approved custom domain).
- Customer designates organization administrators who manage company entities, employee invitations, roles, and statutory rule configurations.
- Customer is responsible for maintaining credential confidentiality and configuring role-based access permissions for its personnel.

## 3. Customer Data & Payroll Responsibility

- Customer retains all ownership rights to Customer Data entered into its tenant workspace, including employee records, attendance events, leave requests, and payroll inputs.
- CMSN-HR executes deterministic payroll, tax, and leave calculations according to active statutory templates and any overrides configured by Customer administrators.
- Customer remains solely responsible for verifying statutory filing obligations, bank disbursements, and jurisdiction-specific labor compliance prior to finalizing payroll runs.

## 4. Subscriptions, Trials & Changes

- Self-service trials run for the duration displayed at signup under the active platform trial plan settings.
- Plan upgrades, renewals, and cancellations follow the active billing terms configured in the Customer billing console.

## 5. Acceptable Use & Security

- Customers may not attempt to bypass tenant isolation controls, rate limits, authentication checks, or access infrastructure belonging to another organization.
- CMSN-HR maintains encryption in transit, field-level encryption for sensitive tokens and identifiers, immutable audit logs for financial actions, and scheduled backups.
