## 1. Data We Collect on the Public Website

- **Trial Signup Data**: Company name, country, owner name, work email address, requested subdomain, and acceptance timestamp and version of the Terms of Service.
- **Sales, Demo & Contact Inquiries**: Name, work email, company name, country, employee headcount band, topic/message, explicit consent selections, and optional campaign attribution parameters.
- **Newsletter & Updates Subscriptions**: Email address, selected feed channel, and timestamped subscription consent.
- **Help Center Feedback**: Anonymous helpfulness votes (`yes` or `no`) without personal profiling.

## 2. Tenant Workspace Processing Roles

- When a Customer uses the CMSN-HR platform to manage employees, attendance, leave, and payroll, the Customer acts as the data controller and CMSN-HR acts as a data processor operating strictly within tenant-isolated boundaries.

## 3. Security Controls & Data Minimization

- Only personal fields needed for workspace provisioning, requested sales follow-up, or explicit subscriptions are persisted.
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt prior to verification completion; verification tokens are stored hashed with short expiration windows.
- Sensitive credentials, banking numbers, and tax identifiers inside tenant workspaces are protected with AES field-level encryption and masked in read interfaces.

## 4. Cookies & Consent

- Necessary storage operates strictly for essential functionality such as currency preference and persisting your cookie consent choice.
- Analytics and marketing scripts remain blocked unless you explicitly opt in via the cookie consent banner or **Cookie Settings**.

## 5. Deletion & Data-Subject Requests

- Public leads, demo requesters, and subscribers may request erasure of their inquiry records via our support/privacy contact channels, processed through audited deletion workflows.
