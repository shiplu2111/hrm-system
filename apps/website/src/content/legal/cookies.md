## 1. How CMSN-HR Uses Cookies & Browser Storage

We organize browser cookies and local storage into three clear categories so you can decide which non-essential categories, if any, may run during your visit.

## 2. Cookie Categories

### Necessary Cookies (Always Active)
Required for core website functionality, security checks, selected display currency (`cmsn_currency`), and remembering your cookie consent decision (`cmsn_cookie_consent`). No optional consent is required for necessary storage, and it cannot be disabled without breaking essential site behavior.

### Analytics Cookies (Optional — Disabled Until Consent)
Used only if you opt in to **Analytics**. Helps measure aggregated page performance and onboarding flow reliability without collecting unrequested personal profile information.

### Marketing Cookies (Optional — Disabled Until Consent)
Used only if you opt in to **Marketing**. Allows preserving campaign source/medium attribution across sessions when evaluating public acquisition channels.

## 3. Managing or Withdrawing Consent

You can accept all categories, decline non-essential categories, or customize Analytics and Marketing individually at any time using the persistent **Cookie Settings** link in the website footer or the control below. When declined or not yet accepted, zero analytics or marketing scripts are loaded and zero tracking requests fire.
